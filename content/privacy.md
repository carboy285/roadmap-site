# Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Roadmap helps students turn assignments into a step-by-step plan. This policy explains what Roadmap collects, why, who else sees it, and how to delete it. Questions go to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

## Who can use Roadmap

Roadmap is for people 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you are under 13, don't create an account. If we learn that an account belongs to a child under 13, we delete it and its data. A parent or guardian who believes this has happened can write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

## What we collect

- **Account.** Your email address and password. The password is stored only in hashed form by our sign-in provider, and we can't read it. If you use Sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the identifier and email those services share.
- **Your plan.** Assignments, course names, instructions you paste or scan, due dates, study steps, progress, study preferences (study days, time window, session length), the first name you enter, and your study history.
- **Google Classroom or Canvas, if you connect them.** Your course list and your own coursework or assignments. Roadmap requests read-only access and never changes anything in Classroom or Canvas. The sign-in tokens for these connections are stored encrypted on our servers.
- **Study materials.** Photos, scans, files and notes you add to an assignment's Study space are read on your iPhone and saved there only. The photos and files themselves aren't uploaded or synced, and they're deleted with the assignment. Roadmap reads the text out of them on your iPhone and uses it to make flashcards, quizzes, summaries and study guides, usually with Apple Intelligence or the offline model on your iPhone. If you have Roadmap Pro and have turned on **Allow cloud AI**, the text (not the images) of the longer pieces can be sent to an approved AI provider through our servers to write them. Our servers pass it on and don't keep it. If you've added your own Groq key, that text is sent to Groq instead.
- **Coach.** The questions you ask, only while cloud AI is on or you've added a Groq key (see below). We also count your daily AI replies to apply the free limit. Your past Coach chats are saved on your iPhone only, so you can reopen them. Delete any chat from the history, or delete your account to remove them all.
- **Subscription.** Whether Roadmap Pro is active, based on App Store receipts. We never see your payment card.
- **Reminders.** If you turn on email reminders, a record of which days a reminder was sent.
- **Notifications.** If you allow notifications, the push token Apple gives your iPhone, so we can tell you when a plan is ready or a deadline is close. The token identifies the app on your device, not you, and is deleted with your account.

Camera and photos are used on your iPhone to read assignment text. The images aren't uploaded. If you tap the microphone to dictate, Apple's speech recognition turns your voice into text. On iPhones that support it this happens on the device, and otherwise Apple processes the audio under its own privacy terms. Roadmap never receives or stores the audio, only the text you send. Your calendar is read on your iPhone to find free time and isn't uploaded. If you turn on calendar sync, Roadmap writes study blocks to a "Roadmap" calendar on your device.

## What we do with it

We use your information to run Roadmap: build and sync your plan, schedule steps, send reminders you asked for, answer Coach questions, and apply subscription benefits. We don't sell your information. We don't show ads. We don't build advertising profiles. Roadmap has no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

## Google user data

Roadmap's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. We use Classroom data only to show and plan your assignments. We don't use it for advertising, we don't sell it, and we don't use it to train AI models. Humans at Roadmap don't read it unless you ask us for help or the law requires it.

## AI

Apple Intelligence runs on your iPhone, and what you give it stays on your iPhone.

Cloud AI is off until you turn on **Allow cloud AI** in Settings. When it's on, Roadmap still uses your iPhone first and sends a request to the cloud only when Apple Intelligence is unavailable or the request is too long for it. Replies that used the cloud are labeled. When a request goes to the cloud, Roadmap sends the relevant assignment text, due dates, progress and your question to an AI provider we have approved, which can include OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Groq or NVIDIA. We use provider plans that don't train on your content, and we won't use a provider that does. Turn the setting off at any time to stop.

**Artwork.** If you have Roadmap Pro and cloud AI is on, you can ask for artwork for a study guide. Roadmap sends a short topic and a summary of the guide to NVIDIA's image service to draw it. Nothing else from your plan is sent.

AI replies can be wrong, so check dates and requirements against your teacher's instructions.

**Offline AI.** On iPhones that can't run Apple Intelligence, Roadmap can download a small open AI model (Qwen, Liquid LFM or Gemma, about 0.8 to 2.3 GB) from Hugging Face over Wi-Fi and run it on your iPhone. Once it's downloaded, your questions and assignments are processed on the iPhone and aren't sent anywhere. Hugging Face can see the download request, including your IP address. You can turn off the automatic download or delete the model in Settings > AI. Replies written by the offline model are labeled.

**Your own Groq key.** If you have Roadmap Pro, you can add your own Groq API key in Settings > AI. When you do, Roadmap sends the same information (the relevant assignment text, due dates, progress and your question) from your iPhone directly to Groq, under your Groq account, whenever Groq answers. Those requests don't pass through Roadmap's servers, and we never receive your key or Groq's replies. The key is stored in your iPhone's Keychain, isn't backed up or synced, and is deleted when you tap Remove key. Groq's own terms and privacy policy govern what Groq does with the content, so read them before you add a key. Replies written by Groq are labeled.

## Sharing with a parent or study buddy

If you sign in, you can make a read-only link in **Settings, Sharing**. Anyone who has the link can see a short summary of your week without an account. For a parent or guardian, that's your first name, your streak, steps finished and focus time this week, assignments due in the next two weeks with the number of steps left, and how many steps are behind schedule. For a study buddy, it's your first name, streak, steps finished and focus time. Links never show grades, assignment instructions, notes, or Coach conversations.

We store only a one-way hash of the link, who it's for, and when it was made. You can stop sharing in the same place, and the link stops working right away. Deleting your account also removes every link. Anyone who has a link can pass it on, so send it only to people you trust.

## Who else handles your data

- **Supabase**, which hosts our database and sign-in.
- **Apple**, for Sign in with Apple, App Store subscriptions and notifications.
- **Google**, for Google sign-in and Classroom.
- **Your school's Canvas**, only if you connect it.
- **An AI provider**, only if you turn on cloud AI.
- **Groq**, only if you add your own Groq key.
- **Resend**, only if you turn on email reminders.
- **Stripe**, only if you subscribe on Roadmap's website instead of in the app. Stripe handles your card, and we never see it.

They process data for us under their own terms and may not use it for their own purposes. We may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

## Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your plan while your account exists. To delete everything, open **Settings, Account, Delete Roadmap account** and type your email to confirm. Deletion removes your plan, Classroom and Canvas connections, notification tokens, share links and subscription records from our systems, and revokes Roadmap's access to your Google and Apple accounts. If you made your account in Roadmap with an email and password, deletion also deletes your sign-in record. If you sign in with Apple or Google, or we can't confirm that only Roadmap uses your sign-in, we don't delete the sign-in record, because other services may share it. We remove Roadmap's data and access, and the record stays with Apple or Google and our sign-in provider. Copies in database backups are overwritten as backups rotate out. You can also email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and we'll delete your account for you.

## Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your plan in the app, disconnect Classroom or Canvas, turn off notifications, turn off cloud AI, turn off reminders and calendar access, and delete your account. Depending on where you live, including under California and EU law, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data. Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and we'll respond within 30 days.

## Security

Data travels over encrypted connections, your sign-in session is kept in the iOS keychain, and database access is restricted to our servers. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we'll notify you as the law requires.

## Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top and tell you in the app the next time you open it. If a change would let us use information you've already given us in a new way, we'll ask for your permission first.
