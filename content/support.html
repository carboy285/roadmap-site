Help with Roadmap for iPhone.

Write to and we’ll reply as soon as we can. Include the iPhone model and iOS version if something isn’t working.

Common questions

Delete my account. Open Settings, then Account, then Delete Roadmap account, and type your email to confirm. You can also ask us to do it for you.

Cancel Roadmap Pro. On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting your Roadmap account does not cancel a subscription, so cancel first. Refunds are handled by Apple.

A due date looks wrong. AI can misread dates. Open the assignment, fix the date, and lock it so the plan keeps it.

A parent or guardian has a concern. Roadmap is for ages 13 and up. If an account belongs to a child under 13, tell us and we’ll delete it.
