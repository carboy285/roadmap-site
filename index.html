How it works

From “due Friday” to a plan in a minute.

  1. Add the assignment

    Type it, paste the instructions, or import a PDF, Word file or photo. Text is read on your iPhone.

  2. Review the steps

    Roadmap breaks it into a checklist you can edit. Always check the title and due date before you save.

  3. Follow your week

    Steps fit into the study days and hours you choose, and dates you lock stay put.

Questions

Who is Roadmap for?

High school students, 13 and older. If you’re under 18, get a parent or guardian’s permission to subscribe.

Do I need an account?

No. The planner works on your iPhone without one. An account adds syncing, Classroom and Canvas, and sharing.

Which iPhones work?

iPhones with iOS 17 or later. Apple Intelligence needs iOS 26 and a supported device. Without it, Roadmap can download a small offline model, or you can plan by hand.

Will Coach do my homework?

No. Roadmap suggests how to work through an assignment, and following your school’s academic integrity rules is up to you. AI can be wrong, so check dates against your teacher’s instructions.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings, then Account, then Delete Roadmap account. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
